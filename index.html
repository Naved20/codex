Frequently asked questions

How much does a private driver in Sri Lanka cost?

Pricing depends on the number of days, vehicle and season. Rates start from €60 per day for an air-conditioned car with fuel included. Share your itinerary for a clear, tailored quote.

Can I hire the same driver for my whole trip?

Yes. Many travellers book one driver for their whole itinerary for an easier, more flexible journey with local recommendations along the way.

Do the drivers speak English?

Our drivers speak conversational English and can help with local recommendations, directions and timing.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. Airport transfers are available from Colombo International Airport to Negombo, Colombo and destinations across Sri Lanka.

Are fuel and driver expenses included?

Fuel and driver fees are included in your agreed price. Any extras, such as entrance tickets or overnight accommodation, are discussed upfront.

Can you help plan my itinerary?

Absolutely. We can suggest a comfortable route, sightseeing stops and the best way to use your time based on the places and experiences you care about.

Is a private driver better than taxis or ride-hailing?

For short trips, taxis and ride-hailing can work. For longer distances and multi-day travel, a private driver offers more reliability, flexibility and convenience.

Can a driver help plan my Sri Lanka itinerary?

Yes. A local driver can suggest efficient routes, sightseeing stops, hidden gems and good times to visit.

Is Sri Lanka suitable for travelling with kids?

Yes. A private driver and comfortable car can make the journey easier for children and older family members.

Can I travel Sri Lanka without a car?

Yes, though it often means combining buses and trains and allowing more travel time. A private driver makes a multi-stop itinerary more direct.

What type of vehicles are used?

We provide air-conditioned, well-maintained vehicles for couples, families and small groups. The vehicle depends on your group size and luggage.

How far in advance should I book?

Booking a few weeks ahead is recommended, especially during peak travel seasons from December to April and July to August.

Are fuel and driver expenses included?

Fuel and driver fees are included. Extras such as entrance tickets or overnight accommodation are discussed clearly in advance.

Why book with My Driver in Sri Lanka?

We offer local drivers, personalized itineraries and a comfortable way to explore the island at your own pace.
